Install on your phone

Runs full screen, keeps you signed in, and is the only way the notifications and the spoken time keep working with the screen off. On iPhone/iPad there is no install button anywhere — open the site in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Chrome and Firefox on iOS cannot do it. On Android use the Install button, or the browser menu.
